            5. Vector Embeddings – Converting Tokens into Numerical Vectors
          

          

            An embedding is a list of numbers called a vector that represents a token. Token IDs are just identification numbers—they do not represent the meaning of a word. Embeddings help AI represent relationships and patterns between words.
          

        

        

          
            
            Click any point to inspect vector
          
        

      


      
      


        
        

          

            

              
                GRAPH 1 · ORTHOGRAPHIC SPACE
              
              8D Character Features → 2D PCA
            

            

              Spelling-Based Vector Embeddings
            

            

              Maps words by letter patterns, prefixes, suffixes, and length. Similar-looking words cluster together.
            


            
            

              
            

          


          

            Spelling-based representations focus on how words look. They capture similarities in letters, character patterns, and spelling.
          

        


        
        

          

            

              
                GRAPH 2 · SEMANTIC SPACE
              
              Semantic Vector → 2D PCA
            

            

              Meaning-Based Vector Embeddings
            

            

              Maps words by conceptual meaning and context. Related concepts cluster together regardless of spelling.
            


            
            

              
            

          


          

            Meaning-based embeddings focus on what words mean rather than how they are spelled. Words with similar meanings are represented by nearby vectors.
